ABOUT HYUNCHAN

HyunChan is the name of the duo formed Hwang Hyunjin and Bang Chan from Stray Kids.

In 2021, they released their first song together, "Red Lights." After its release, they began to gain more attention and became known among fans as the "Red Lights duo".

In March 2025, Hyunjin and Bang Chan released their second unit song, "ESCAPE." Fans were extremely excited to see them together as a unit again and the duo received even more attention.

HyunChan is loved by fans for their chemistry, contrasting personalities, and memorable performancesan. The cutest and at the same time sexiest duo.
